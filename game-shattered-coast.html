“Everything I’ve done has been a way to make something. I’ve drawn and painted my whole life, on paper and on screens. Barbering is art. Surfing is art, and so is every dream wave I’ve sketched since I was twelve. Shattered Coast is the newest expression of that same lifelong need: to take how something feels and hand it to someone else. It’s a living version of Tofino, the town I called home for ten years, and the waves are shaped by twenty-two years of reading the ocean. AI is the paintbrush I finally found. It writes the code, and I check every line, but the world, the story and the waves come from a place no tool can reach. This is my attempt to capture the magic of surfing and share it with anyone who’s ever wanted to feel it.”

— Darren Lundquist, Founder
