Privacy policy

This page is the home for the Don't Send Help privacy policy. Paste the store version here when it is final.

What the game does on your machine

Don't Send Help is a single-player surf sandbox from Shattered Coast Interactive. Saves stay on the device you play on, unless a store platform adds its own account layer.

What we do not do

  • No account with us.
  • No sale of personal information.
  • No ads inside the game.

Store platforms

If you buy the game on Steam or another store, that store has its own privacy policy for purchases, friends lists, and cloud saves. That policy is theirs, not ours.
