Don't Send Help

Privacy policy

Effective October 26, 2026. This policy covers the iPhone version of Don't Send Help, published by Shattered Coast Interactive Limited.

Gameplay and saved progress

The game does not require an account with us. Your settings, game position, cumulative points and exploration progress are saved locally on your device. The game does not send these saves to us or provide its own cloud-sync service. Removing the app may remove its local saves.

Data collection and tracking

The iPhone game does not collect or transmit personal information to Shattered Coast Interactive Limited or an analytics or advertising service. It contains no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics. We do not sell player information. The game does not request your real-world location, contacts, microphone or camera for gameplay.

App Store purchases

Apple handles purchases, including optional Support the Artist tips, through its payment system. We do not receive your payment-card details. The game stores a limited purchase transaction history on your device to avoid showing duplicate thank-you messages. Tips provide no gameplay advantage or unlock.

Apple may process purchase, account and diagnostic information under its own policies. See Apple's privacy policy. Apple's App Store and TestFlight services are separate from the game's local saves.

Support and questions

If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to include so we can respond. Please do not send passwords or payment-card information.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date if the iPhone game's privacy practices change.
